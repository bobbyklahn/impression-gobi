The Story

An estate winery in Changji. Not a slogan on a bottle from somewhere else.

昌吉 · 三工
SCROLL
EST. 2014
— Beginning

Changji sits where the Gobi softens into farmland. In 2014 we registered the company and started planting for wine, not for volume alone.

The name is simple: Impression Gobi — what this desert leaves on the palate when you grow Cabernet and Marselan the long way.

02 / Years

A short timeline.

Only the dates that matter. No invented folklore.

2014

Company founded

Changji City Impression Gobi Winery Co., Ltd. Legal representative Fu Qiang. Base in Sangong Town, Changji — Tian Shan Beilu small region.

2014–2018

Vines in, cellar working

Plantings of Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot, Syrah, Marselan, Pinot Noir, and a few white varieties. First serious bottlings find their feet. A 2018 Cabernet–Merlot–Syrah later takes a Decanter World Wine Awards bronze — herb, pepper, structured tannin.

2016 onward

Organic path

Dry climate means fewer sprays. We push organic certification on key blocks and keep farming that way. It is not a trend story. It is how this land behaves.

2022

France, three golds

Three wines entered at a spring French international competition come back with gold. Useful proof for people who have never heard of Changji.

2024

IWSC Asia judges on site

International Wine & Spirit Competition judges walk the estate and taste in Changji. The house stays open to people who actually taste for a living.

Today

Estate + door open

Own vineyards, cellar, and a small hospitality side. Chief winemaker Li Zhendong steers the technical work. We still farm first, talk second.

03 / People

Who runs the house.

General Manager

Fu Qiang 富强

Leads the company day to day. Trade, estate visits, and the long job of getting Xinjiang wine taken seriously outside the region.

Chief Winemaker

Li Zhendong 李振东
