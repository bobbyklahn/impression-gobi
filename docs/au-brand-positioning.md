## 1. Brand positioning (AU)

### One-liner
> Estate reds from the Gobi edge of the Tian Shan — organic blocks, real structure, a story Australians have never tasted.

### Positioning statement
For **curious Australian drinkers and independent trade** who already love Barossa heat and firm tannin, **Impression Gobi** is the **Chinese desert estate** that delivers **herb, pepper, and structured reds** from **organic plantings in Changji** — not a soft import novelty, but a **place-first house** they can put next to McLaren Vale and still hold the table.

### Not competing with
- Everyday Dan Murphy house Chinese blends  
- Ningxia “Bordeaux clone” storytelling alone  
- Cheap gift reds  

### Competing for
- Specialty shelf “discovery”  
- Asian fine dining by-the-glass  
- Trade tastings where **Marselan** and **CMS structure** win the second pour  

### Brand personality
| Trait | In practice |
|-------|-------------|
| Honest | No invented folklore; real 2014 founding, real medals |
| Desert-hard | Structure over soft fruit |
| Quiet confidence | Gecko mark, few words |
| Host, not lecturer | Talk food first |

## 3. Market wine talkers (staff / trade)

### 20-second opener (any SKU)
> “This is Impression Gobi — estate wine from Changji, north of the Tian Shan in Xinjiang. Desert light, cold nights, organic blocks. If you like structure, start here.”

### SKU 01 — Cabernet · Merlot · Syrah
**Shelf talker (print):**
```
IMPRESSION GOBI
Cabernet · Merlot · Syrah
Tian Shan Beilu · Changji

Herb. Pepper. Real tannin.
The house red — not jam.

Pair: rib-eye · lamb · aged cheddar
```

**Staff notes:**  
- Style bridge: Barossa / McLaren Vale drinkers  
- DWWA bronze on 2018 cuvée (herbaceous, pepper, structured)  
- Lead pour for first-timers  

### SKU 02 — Marselan blend
**Shelf talker:**
```
IMPRESSION GOBI
Marselan Blend
Xinjiang’s signature grape

Blackberry · plum · plush mid-palate
Ask for the Marselan.

Pair: char siu · five-spice duck · hard cheese
```

**Staff notes:**  
- “China’s grape Australians should learn next”  
- Softer entry than CMS; great by-the-glass  

### SKU 03 — Organic cuvées
**Shelf talker:**
```
IMPRESSION GOBI
Organic Cuvées
Certified blocks · Cabernet & Merlot

Smoke · black cherry · clean finish
Farming first. Medals second.

Pair: roast chicken · mushroom pie · soft goat
```

**Staff notes:**  
- Organic is climate-backed (low rain, low disease)  
- For buyers who ask about farming before scores  

### Objection handlers
| They say | You say |
|----------|---------|
| “Chinese wine?” | “Same latitude band as Mediterranean wine country — different desert. Taste first.” |
| “Too tannic” | “That’s the Gobi. Air and food fix it — try with lamb.” |
| “Never heard of Xinjiang” | “North of the Tian Shan. Think Silk Road, then open the bottle.” |
| “Price vs Barossa?” | “You’re paying for discovery + estate farming, not a famous postcode.” |
